How Modmail Works:

  1. A member sends a Direct Message to the SRB NEXUS bot.
  2. The bot posts an inbound card with user details and message to your configured staff channel.
  3. A staff member replies in Discord using /modmail reply user:<id> message:<text>.
  4. The bot sends the response straight into the user's DM anonymously on behalf of server staff!
